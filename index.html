About

Quality Engineer with 5+ years across hardware and embedded-systems QA — PCBA testing, failure analysis, incoming inspection, and test automation. I'm the person who looks at a manual, repetitive QA process and asks whether it should be a script instead.

Most of my work sits where quality meets automation. I've converted manual test and inspection workflows into Python tooling, built dashboards and inspection systems on Google Apps Script, and led ISO 9001 quality-system work. Recently I designed and deployed an AI agent that automatically flags likely-duplicate defects in Jira by reasoning about root cause rather than keywords — a working, human-in-the-loop tool, not a prototype.

My background spans electronics and communication engineering (B.E.) and a post-graduate diploma in Embedded Systems Engineering Design. I'm strongest where hardware, test data, and a bit of code all meet — and I care about building things that actually get used.
